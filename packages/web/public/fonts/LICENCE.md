# Fonts

All three faces are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits bundling and
serving them from our own origin.

- Chivo, by Omnibus-Type. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Chivo
- Hanken Grotesk, by Hanken Design Co. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Hanken+Grotesk
- JetBrains Mono, by JetBrains. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono

Each file here is the latin subset only, taken from the Google Fonts release. They are served from
our own origin so no request leaves the site, which keeps the content security policy in
`infra/site/security-headers.tf` closed to third parties.
