# Positioning

Decided 18 August 2026. Read this before you write a page.

## The company

ATLANTIC BLUE SOLUTIONS LTD. Company number 14468993. Registered at 86-90 Paul Street, London,
EC2A 4NE. Incorporated on 8 November 2022. A consultancy, not a product company.

## The offer

Atlantic Blue is a London consultancy for broadcasters and streamers. It builds video playback,
streaming delivery, and the reliability work that keeps a service on air.

General software engineering is the second offer. It is real work and we take it. It is not the
headline, because it does not win anything.

## The buyer

An engineering manager or a head of platform inside a broadcaster or a streaming service. They are
usually one of these people:

- Their player fails on one family of devices and nobody on the team owns playback.
- A live event is coming and they do not know whether the platform holds.
- Buffering complaints are rising and the adaptive bitrate logic is somebody else's code.
- Their advertising integration breaks playback, or their advertising revenue leaks.
- They need a specialist for three months and do not want an agency of twelve.

They buy a named person with a track record. They do not buy a team of unnamed developers.

## Why this position, and not the old one

The previous site sold "tailored software development solutions in London". It cannot win that.

Measured on 18 August 2026, competitors in that phrase run 29 to 302 pages, publish structured data,
name their clients and carry years of reviews. Square Root Solutions alone reaches 58 pages from its
home page and 6 types of structured data. Atlantic Blue reached 1 page and none. Entering that race
from one page is not a plan.

Broadcasting and streaming is a race almost nobody else has entered. The work is specialised, the
buyers are few, they pay well, and a generic consultancy cannot reach them. Atlantic Blue has nine
years of it and the clients to prove it.

## The relationship with juliantellez.com

Two sites name the same clients. Without a rule they compete for one identity in search, and each
weakens the other.

- juliantellez.com is the person. Curriculum vitae, blog, projects, the Built from Scratch
  newsletter, availability for contract work.
- atlanticblue.solutions is the company. Services, case studies, engagement models, pricing, the
  invoicing entity.

Each site links to the other and says what it is. The company site marks up Organization and
LocalBusiness. The personal site owns the Person entity. Neither republishes the other's writing. A
subject belongs to one site only, and the other links to it.

## Voice

Write in Simplified Technical English. Short sentences. Active voice. One word for one meaning.

Banned everywhere on this site: elevate, cutting edge, seamless, robust, comprehensive, leverage,
best in class, game changer, unlock, empower. The old copy used five of them in two paragraphs.

Every claim carries a number or a named client, or it does not go on the page.
